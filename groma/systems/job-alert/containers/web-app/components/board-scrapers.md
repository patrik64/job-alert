description: One scraper per tracked fund, registered by slug
---

The registry maps every fund slug in the catalogue to the scraper of its board, and the contract every scraper meets: list the board's jobs, and optionally read one job's detail. Most entries are one line configuring a shared platform reader with the board's host and id; the rest are bespoke readers for fund-run pages, CMS listings, Algolia indexes or company-by-company crawls. A scraper fails loudly rather than return a partial list, because missing jobs would be deleted and later return as false newcomers.
